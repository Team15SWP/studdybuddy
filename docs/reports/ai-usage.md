# AI Usage Report

## Week 1
During the first week, we used AI exclusively to refine the layout and structure of our assignment. It helped ensure the formatting met academic standards, but all content was written independently.

## Week 2
This week, AI assisted us only in formatting headings, aligning bullet points, and making sure sections followed a consistent style. It was not used for generating any original content.

## Week 3
We relied on AI solely to enhance the visual presentation of our assignment, such as fixing indentation, aligning tables, and adjusting font styles. All the actual solutions and explanations were our own.

## Week 4
AI helped this week by organizing the structure of the document and standardizing formatting (e.g., code blocks, references). Its use was limited to appearance improvements only.

## Week 5
The only role AI played was to polish formatting — such as ensuring proper line spacing, font consistency, and paragraph alignment. All the ideas and implementation were manually done.

## Week 6
AI was utilized for cleaning up the formatting of the assignment. It helped in applying consistent styling rules across the document but was not involved in any decision-making or content writing.

## Week 7
In the final week, we used AI simply to correct visual inconsistencies in the assignment, such as fixing section headers and standardizing code layout. All intellectual work was completed without AI.
